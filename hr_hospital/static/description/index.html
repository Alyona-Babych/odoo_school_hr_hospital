Hospital Management

Comprehensive hospital workflow automation for Odoo 19

The hr_hospital module provides tools for managing doctors, patients, diseases, and patient visits within the hospital environment. The module helps organize medical workflows, appointments, doctor assignments, visit history, and reporting processes.

Main features:

  • Doctors and patients management
  • Medical visit scheduling and tracking
  • Disease classification with hierarchical structure
  • Doctor qualification categories and internship management
  • Personal doctor assignment history
  • Medical reports and analytical views
  • Calendar, kanban, pivot, graph, and search views
  • Wizard-based mass operations and reporting tools
  • Access rights management for different hospital roles
  • QWeb printable reports
  • Ukrainian translation support

This module was developed as a practical training project for learning Odoo 19 backend development concepts including models, views, security, wizards, reports, and business logic implementation.
